# Outreach CRM — Acceptable Use Policy

Version {{version}} · Effective {{effective_date}}

Outreach CRM is built for **legitimate, relevant business-to-business outreach**. This policy forms part of the [Terms of Use](terms). Breaking it ends your licence to use the Software.

## You must

- Contact people only at work addresses, about matters relevant to their job, where you have a lawful basis to do so in their country.
- Use only contact data that you are legally entitled to use for email outreach, and follow the terms of the data source.
- Send from an address and name that truthfully identify you and your business, and include your business's postal address (the Software's footer does this).
- Use subject lines that accurately reflect the message.
- Give every recipient a simple way to opt out, and honour opt-outs promptly and permanently. The Software stops emailing anyone who replies asking to be removed, and adds bounced addresses to the do-not-contact list.
- Review messages before they are sent, including text written by AI.
- Keep sending volumes modest and within your email provider's limits.

## You must not

- Send messages that are unlawful, fraudulent, deceptive, harassing, threatening, defamatory, hateful or sexually explicit.
- Send phishing messages, malware, or links designed to deceive.
- Impersonate any person or organisation, or falsify sender details or message headers.
- Contact anyone who has opted out, or use the Software to evade another service's blocks or limits.
- Use the Software to contact consumers at personal addresses for marketing, or contact anyone in a country where your messages require prior consent you do not have.
- Collect or store sensitive personal data (such as health, financial or government ID information) about contacts.
- Use the Software to violate any third-party service's terms, including your email provider's.

## Laws you must follow

You are responsible for complying with the laws of your country and of your recipients' countries. Examples include:

- **Pakistan**: the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016, as amended, which prohibits spamming.
- **United States**: the CAN-SPAM Act — accurate headers and subject lines, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days.
- **European Union and United Kingdom**: the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the ePrivacy rules (in the UK, PECR), which set when business emails need consent and require a lawful basis for processing personal data.
- **Canada**: Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), which generally requires consent before sending commercial email.
- **Australia**: the Spam Act 2003, which generally requires consent before sending commercial email. Outreach CRM excludes Australia from lead searches by default for this reason.

This list is not complete and is not legal advice. If you are unsure whether a campaign is lawful, get advice from a qualified lawyer before sending.
